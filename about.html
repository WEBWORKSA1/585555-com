About 585555

58 reads as 我发 — "I prosper". 5555 sounds like crying. We think that's the honest story of every business: prosperity, earned through hard days.

What we do

We build free tools and plain-English guides that help businesses and creators understand how Chinese-speaking customers read numbers, colours, dates and gifts — and turn that understanding into better prices, launches and campaigns.

Principles

  • Useful first. Every page must help someone make a decision.
  • Respectful. Culture is not a costume. We explain the why and note regional differences.
  • Honest. Lucky numbers are cultural signals, not guarantees. We say so.
  • Free. Funded by clearly labelled ads, sponsors and supporters.

Editorial standards

Guides are written and reviewed by the 585555 Editorial Team, cite sources where facts are stated, and are updated regularly. Spot an error? Tell us.
