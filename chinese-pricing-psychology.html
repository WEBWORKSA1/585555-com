Chinese pricing psychology: why ¥88 beats ¥90

By the 585555 Editorial Team · Updated {{ site.time | date: "%B %Y" }} · 7 min read

A price is a number, and in Chinese a number is also a word. Get the word right and your price does double duty: it anchors value and sends a blessing.

1. Two layers of meaning

Western retail relies on left-digit bias: $9.99 feels much cheaper than $10. Chinese-speaking shoppers experience that too, but they also hear the price. Mandarin and Cantonese are full of homophones, so digits become syllables of meaning:

  • 8 (bā) ≈ 发 fā — prosper
  • 6 (liù) ≈ 流 liú — flow smoothly
  • 9 (jiǔ) ≈ 久 jiǔ — long-lasting
  • 4 (sì) ≈ 死 sǐ — death

2. The strongest endings

Endings get remembered. In practice the hierarchy is: 888 › 88 › 8 › 68/168 › 66/99 › 6/9. Premium goods lean on repeated eights (¥888, ¥1,888, ¥8,888). Everyday goods use a single 8 (¥18, ¥28, ¥38 — with care, see below).

4. Promotional numbers that sell

  • 168 一路发 — "prosper all the way": bundles, New Year sets.
  • 518 我要发 — "I will prosper": opening-day offers.
  • 520 / 1314 — "I love you" / "a lifetime": jewellery, weddings, 20 May.
  • 666 六六大顺 — "everything smooth": also youth slang for "awesome".

5. A repricing checklist

  1. Export your price list and flag every price containing 4 or 250.
  2. Run each flagged price through the Lucky Price Optimizer.
  3. Prefer the nearest-above option for premium lines and nearest-below for traffic drivers.
  4. Create one "lucky SKU" per category (a 168 or 888 bundle) for Lunar New Year and 11.11.
  5. Match packaging: red/gold for gifts, and quantities of 2, 6 or 8.

6. Does it actually lift sales?

Evidence from asset markets is strong: auctions of licence plates and phone numbers in Hong Kong, mainland China and Malaysia consistently price 8s higher and 4s lower, and property studies in Chinese-majority markets find discounts for 4-numbered units and floors. For everyday retail, treat lucky pricing as a low-cost signal of cultural respect — test it with A/B pricing where your platform allows.
