Colours in Chinese marketing

By the 585555 Editorial Team · Updated {{ site.time | date: "%B %Y" }} · 6 min read

Colour is the first thing a customer reads — before your headline, before your price.

ColourSignalsUse forCaution
RedLuck, joy, celebration, protectionFestivals, weddings, gifts, CTAsRed ink for names is taboo; red in finance charts in China means prices up
GoldWealth, statusPremium, New Year, accentsOveruse feels gaudy
YellowImperial power, earthHeritage brands黄 can also mean "pornographic" in slang
WhiteMourning, purityMinimal tech/healthAvoid for gifts, envelopes and festive creative
BlackFormality, sophistication; also misfortuneLuxury accentsNot as a festive base
GreenHealth, growth, natureFood, wellness, ecoNever a green hat
BlueTrust, calm, technologyB2B, fintechCan feel cold in festive contexts

A quick recipe

  1. Keep your brand colours year-round.
  2. Add a red-and-gold "festival skin" for Lunar New Year and Mid-Autumn.
  3. Avoid white envelopes and white flowers entirely in gifting.
  4. In stock or finance creative for mainland audiences, remember red = rising.
