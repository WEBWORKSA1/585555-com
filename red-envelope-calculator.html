Red Envelope Calculator

The right amount, snapped to lucky numbers, for every occasion — personal and business.

The five golden rules

  1. Even amounts for happy occasions ("good things come in pairs"). Odd amounts are for funerals.
  2. No 4s anywhere — not $40, not $140, not $400.
  3. Favour 8, 6 and 9: $88, $168, $188, $288, $388, $888.
  4. New, crisp notes. Visit the bank before New Year.
  5. Give and receive with both hands, and don't open an envelope in front of the giver.

Red envelopes at work

In Hong Kong, Singapore and Malaysia, employers commonly give lai see to staff, cleaners and building security after New Year. Married managers are expected to give to unmarried juniors. For businesses entering these markets, a written hongbao policy avoids awkwardness — our free growth plan includes one.

FAQ

How much for a wedding?
A common rule is at least the cost of your seat at the banquet, rounded to a lucky figure. Close family give substantially more.
Digital red envelopes?
WeChat and other apps cap single digital red packets (WeChat's classic cap is ¥200), so large gifts are often sent as multiple packets or transfers. Lucky amounts like ¥88 and ¥168 are popular.
