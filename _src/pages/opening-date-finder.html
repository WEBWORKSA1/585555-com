The rules we apply

  • God of Wealth Day (5th day of the 1st lunar month, 破五): the traditional day businesses reopen after New Year. Rated highest.
  • Lunar New Year period (days 1–15 of the 1st lunar month): festive and favourable.
  • Ghost Month (7th lunar month): many customers postpone big purchases, weddings and moves. Avoid openings.
  • Qingming (around 4–5 April): tomb-sweeping; avoid celebrations.
  • Digits: lunar or calendar dates containing 8 (and 6, 9) score up; those containing 4 score down.
This tool applies widely held popular customs. It is not a Tung Shing almanac reading. Families and businesses that follow a specific master or almanac should confirm with them.

FAQ

When is Ghost Month in 2027?
The 7th lunar month runs from 2 August to 31 August 2027 (it falls 13 August – 10 September in 2026).
When is God of Wealth Day 2027?
Wednesday 10 February 2027 — five days after Lunar New Year on 6 February 2027.
