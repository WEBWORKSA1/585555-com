You don't need to believe in lucky numbers. Your customers, tenants and partners might — and that's enough to make them a business variable.

Key combinations

  • 28 — easy prosperity (Cantonese). A Hong Kong plate "28" sold for HK$18.1 million in 2016.
  • 58 — I prosper. 518 — I will prosper. 168 — prosper all the way.
  • 88 / 888 / 8888 — compounding prosperity.
  • 14, 24, 74, 514, 748 — variants of death or anger. Avoid.

Where it matters most

Real estate

Many towers in Hong Kong, mainland China, Singapore, Vancouver and Toronto skip 4th, 14th and 24th floors. Units numbered with 8 can command premiums with Chinese buyers. If you market property, check unit and address numbers before listing.

Phone numbers

Phone numbers are one of the cheapest places to buy luck. A famous example: Sichuan Airlines paid around ¥2.33 million in 2003 for the Chengdu number 8888-8888. Most businesses can get a line ending in 88 or 168 for very little.

Dates

The 2008 Beijing Olympics opened on 8/8/08 at 8:08 pm. Launch dates matter — use the Opening Date Finder.

Domains & handles

Numbers are easier to type than pinyin for many Chinese users, which is why short numeric .com names are in demand. See why numeric domains sell.
