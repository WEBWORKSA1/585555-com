The Lunar New Year marketing playbook — 2027, Year of the Goat

By the 585555 Editorial Team · Updated {{UPDATED}} · 9 min read

Lunar New Year 2027 begins on Saturday 6 February. It's the Year of the Fire Goat (丁未) — also called Sheep or Ram. Here's how to plan it from zero.

The timeline

WhenWhat to do
OctoberBrief creative; order zodiac packaging and red envelopes; lock budgets.
NovemberBuild lucky-number bundles (168, 888); plan influencer content.
DecemberPre-launch teasers; corporate gifting orders; staff hongbao policy.
Mid-JanuaryCampaign live. Last shipping dates around Little New Year (30 Jan 2027).
6–20 FebFestival period through Lantern Festival (20 Feb). Relaunch on God of Wealth Day, 10 Feb.

Creative do's

  • Red and gold palette; auspicious phrases like 恭喜发财 (wishing you prosperity) and 新年快乐 (happy new year).
  • Family reunion, abundance, fresh starts. Goat themes: gentleness, creativity, harmony.
  • Pairs and eights in product counts and prices.

Creative don'ts

  • No white or black dominant designs; no 4s in prices or countdowns.
  • Don't mix up zodiac animals or use a generic "Chinese" font as costume — hire native reviewers.
  • Avoid Japanese or Korean imagery as a stand-in; markets notice.
  • Don't run clearance ("everything must go") messaging — it evokes loss at a time of abundance.

Promotions that convert

  1. Digital hongbao: randomised discount envelopes (e.g. $8, $18, $88 off).
  2. Lucky bundles: a "168 set" priced at $168 or $16.80.
  3. Zodiac personalisation: "find your animal" quiz feeding product recommendations.
  4. Reunion gifting: ship-to-family options with red wrap.
  5. God of Wealth Day relaunch on 10 Feb — perfect for new menus, product drops and store reopenings.

Measure it

Track conversion by audience segment, average order value on lucky bundles vs. regular SKUs, and red-envelope code redemption. Compare with the same period last year adjusted for the shifting lunar date (Lunar New Year 2026 was 17 February).
