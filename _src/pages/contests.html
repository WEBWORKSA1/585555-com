Official rules (summary)

Eligibility
Open worldwide to individuals aged 18+, except where prohibited by law. Employees and contractors of 585555.com and their immediate families are not eligible.
How to enter
Submit one original poster (PNG/JPG/PDF, min. 2000px) via the form before the deadline shown above. One entry per person. No purchase or donation is necessary, and donating does not improve your chances.
Judging
Entries are judged by a panel on creativity (40%), cultural accuracy (30%) and visual quality (30%). Winners are notified by email within 14 days of the deadline and must respond within 10 days.
Prizes
First prize US$585; two runner-up prizes of US$88 each; paid by PayPal or bank transfer. Winners are responsible for any taxes. Prizes are non-transferable. If fewer than 10 valid entries are received, the organiser may extend the deadline.
