How the score is calculated

  1. Digit weights. Each digit adds or subtracts points: 8 (+8), 6 and 9 (+5), 2 (+3), 3 (+2), 0/1/5 (+1), 7 (0), 4 (−10).
  2. Combinations. Known phrases such as 168, 518, 888, 1314 add points; 14, 24, 74, 250 and 748 subtract.
  3. Ending. The last digit carries extra weight — people remember endings.
  4. Repeats. Runs of three or more good digits (666, 888) add a bonus.

Scores above 70 are "very auspicious"; below 40 we suggest changing the number where it's cheap to do so (a phone line or suite letter is easier to change than a street address).

FAQ

Mandarin or Cantonese?
We blend both. Where they differ (e.g. 28 "easy prosperity" is a Cantonese reading; 38 can be an insult in Mandarin but "prosper in life" in Cantonese), we note it.
Is a low score "bad luck"?
No. It measures how customers are likely to perceive the number — a marketing signal, not a prediction.
