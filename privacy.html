What we collect

How forms are delivered

Form submissions are relayed by a third-party form service (FormSubmit) to our private inbox. That service processes the data solely to deliver it.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You may opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. See how Google uses data from partner sites.

Analytics

With consent, we use Google Analytics to understand aggregate usage.

Embedded content

YouTube videos load in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and only after you click play.

Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your data via our contact form. Where GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, CCPA/CPRA or similar laws apply, we honour the rights they give you.

Retention & security

We keep inquiry data only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable business records. We never sell personal data.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in the EU), and contests are for adults 18+.
