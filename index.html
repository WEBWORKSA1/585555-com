Why numbers move money

Lucky numbers are not trivia. They are priced.

In 2016 a Hong Kong licence plate reading simply "28" (易发, "easy prosperity" in Cantonese) sold at a government auction for HK$18.1 million (about US$2.3 million). Researchers have measured premiums for 8-heavy plates, phone numbers and apartment floors across Chinese markets, and discounts for 4.

If your customers read 8 as "prosper" and 4 as "death", your price tag, phone number and launch date are already sending a message. Our tools make sure it's the right one.

What does 585555 mean?
Read as Mandarin sounds, 5 (wǔ) ≈ 我 "I" and 8 (bā) ≈ 发 "prosper", so 58 = "I prosper". 5555 (wūwūwūwū) is internet slang for crying. Together: "I prosper — through the tears." Full breakdown.
