Lucky Price Optimizer

Swap charm pricing for prosperity pricing. Enter a price, get the nearest auspicious alternatives your Chinese-speaking customers will notice.

How it works

The optimizer searches roughly 12% below to 15% above your price. It keeps only candidates that (1) contain no digit 4, and (2) end in a strong digit or pair — 8, 88, 68, 98, 66 or 99. Each candidate is scored with our number engine and ranked by score minus distance from your original price, so you don't give away margin for luck.

Why lucky pricing works

Western "charm pricing" ($9.99) exploits left-digit bias. Chinese-speaking shoppers respond to a second layer: the sound of the number. 8 (bā) sounds like 发 (fā, "to prosper"); 6 (liù) like 流 ("smooth flow"); 9 (jiǔ) like 久 ("long-lasting"). A menu set at $18.88 signals care; one at $14.44 signals the opposite.

FAQ

Does this work for non-Chinese customers too?
Yes — prices ending in 8 still read as deliberate and premium. There's little downside with Western shoppers and clear upside with Chinese-speaking ones.
Should I remove every 4?
On prices, suite numbers and phone numbers aimed at Chinese customers, yes where practical. A 4 inside a long SKU or barcode matters far less.
Is there a "best" lucky price?
Endings of 8, 88 and 888 are the strongest; 168 ("prosper all the way") and 518 ("I will prosper") are classic promotional numbers.
