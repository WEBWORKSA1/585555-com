Presentation matters as much as the gift

  • Wrap in red or gold. Avoid white, black and blue wrapping.
  • Give in pairs or eights, never sets of four.
  • Expect polite refusal once or twice before a gift is accepted — offer again.
  • Hand over with both hands, and let the recipient open it later.
  • Public-sector rules: officials in mainland China face strict gift limits; keep gifts modest and branded.
